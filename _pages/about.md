I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Engineering at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT). My research focuses on enhancing hardware security for machine learning models through innovative Side-Channel Analysis (SCA) techniques. My work leverages power analysis to detect potential security violations in ML models and investigate adversarial attacks through power consumption patterns.

I received my BSc in Electrical Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Sylhet, Bangladesh, in 2022.

## Research Focus

**Primary Research Areas:** Hardware Security and Digital VLSI/CAD
